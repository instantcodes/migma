Play & Save Video

Paste a video link. It plays here and is recorded. You can pause, stop and download the result at any time.

Direct video files (.mp4, .webm, .ogg) are recorded automatically. For embed or page links, click Record player, choose This tab and tick Share tab audio; only the player area is recorded (Chrome or Edge). If the network drops or the video freezes or is paused, the recording pauses by itself and continues when playback returns. Pause recording holds it until you resume. Stop recording ends it, and Download final recording saves the finished file, also after an early stop. Use the Pause video button here rather than the player’s own controls so the page always knows the state. Protected (DRM) video records as a black screen. Only save videos you have the right to keep.
