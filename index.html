Direct video files (.mp4, .webm, .ogg) are recorded at their own resolution. For embed or page links, first set the player’s quality (gear icon) to the highest option, then click Record player, choose This tab and tick Share tab audio. The player then enlarges to fill the window, a 3 second countdown runs, and only the player area is recorded (Chrome or Edge). Actual resolution and frame rate depend on your window size, screen and computer; the real values are shown while recording. If the network drops or the video freezes, the recording pauses by itself and continues when playback returns. Download final recording saves the finished file, also after an early stop. Every start, pause, resume, network loss and stop is listed in the timeline below the player and can be saved as a CSV file. Protected (DRM) video records as a black screen. Only save videos you have the right to keep.
